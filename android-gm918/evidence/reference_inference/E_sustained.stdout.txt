**Technical Explanation of Transformer Inference on ARM64 Phones**

Transformer inference is a powerful approach to natural language processing (NLP) that relies on the **self-attention mechanism** to model relationships between words in a sentence. When deployed on resource-constrained devices like ARM64 phones, the challenge is to optimize the model for efficiency, while maintaining performance and accuracy.

---

### 1. **Tokenization**

Tokenization is the process of converting text into a sequence of tokens (e.g., words, subwords, or character-level units) that can be processed by the model. In the context of transformer inference:

- **Input Tokenization**: The text is tokenized into a sequence of tokens, typically using a tokenizer like BPE (Byte Pair Encoding) or WordPiece.
- **Padding and Truncation**: To handle variable-length inputs, the sequence is padded to a fixed length and truncated if necessary.
- **Efficiency on ARM64**: ARM64 processors support efficient tokenization with lightweight libraries like **onnxruntime** or **TensorRT**, which optimize for speed and memory usage.
